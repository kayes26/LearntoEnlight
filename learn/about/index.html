Online Maths & Cadet College Admission Coaching

Learn to Enlight is an independent online coaching program for students in Bangladesh, founded by Md. Kayes. I started the program to help students build a strong foundation in mathematics and prepare for Cadet College admission through regular practice and feedback. Our Class 5 and Class 6 batches combine live classes, timed tests, and homework. I review students’ work and use their mistakes to decide what to revisit in class.

Families can also enquire about support in Mathematics, Science, and ICT up to SSC or O Level equivalent.

Meet Md. Kayes

Meet Md. Kayes

Founder and Lead Instructor

I’m a mathematics instructor with over 12 years of teaching experience, including online teaching at Mojaru. I’m a former cadet of Cumilla Cadet College, earned a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), and am pursuing a master’s degree at the University of Dhaka. Having gone through Cadet College admission myself, I understand the preparation it takes and the pressure students can feel along the way.

Alongside teaching, I work in ClickUp, workflow automation, and business operations. I use that experience to organise recordings, notes, homework, and exam feedback in the student portal and keep parents informed through Telegram updates.
