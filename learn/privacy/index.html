Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you enquire, book a consultation, apply, pay for a course, or take part in our classes.

Learn to Enlight provides online coaching for students in Bangladesh. This policy explains how we handle information when you enquire, book a consultation, apply, pay for a course, or take part in our classes.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect the student’s name, class or batch, and the parent or guardian’s name and contact details, including a mobile or WhatsApp number where provided. The admission form currently also requires a full home address. We use this address to deliver gifts or rewards to students. We keep records related to enrollment and teaching, such as attendance, homework, exam results, instructor feedback, and payment references. If you contact us or book a consultation, we receive the details you submit.

The Class Join page asks for a batch and student name to search for a matching record and record attendance. The page code reviewed does not request an IP address; whether n8n, hosting, or another service retains IP or other technical request logs is not documented in this project.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations, manage enrollment, verify payments, deliver classes and learning materials, track student progress, and send updates to parents or guardians. Students can access their dashboard and available class recordings while their enrollment is active. Parents or guardians receive progress and attendance updates.

Services we use

The current site uses Cal.com for bookings, ClickUp forms and n8n webhooks for enquiries, student records, attendance, and workflows, Google Meet for classes, and Telegram and WhatsApp for contact and updates. The payment page currently presents bKash, Nagad, Cellfin, and Islami Bank transfer options; the relevant payment provider or bank processes payment details. We use payment status and transaction references or other submitted payment details to match a payment to the correct enrollment. These services may handle information under their own privacy terms.

Students under 18

The admission form instructs that a parent or legal guardian should complete it on behalf of the student. Its single required consent checkbox says: “I confirm that I am the student’s parent or legal guardian. I agree to the Terms & Conditions and consent to the use of the student’s information as described in the Privacy Policy.”

How long we keep information

We keep information as needed to provide coaching, manage enrollment and payments, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can contact us about information we still hold after enrollment ends.

Your choices and how to contact us

You can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or withdraw consent where applicable. Parents and legal guardians can also contact us about their child’s information. Some records may need to be kept for legal or payment recordkeeping.

For privacy questions or requests, use the chat option on our website or book a meeting with us.
