Welcome to Learn with Kayes

We want every student and parent to understand how our classes, payments, learning resources, and program policies work. Please read these terms before completing admission.

A flexible way to enroll

If you are unsure about continuing for several months, we recommend choosing the monthly payment option. You can decide on your next month without committing to a longer payment period.

Applies to: Students and parents/guardians enrolling in Learn with Kayes online learning programs.

1. About the program

  • Learn with Kayes operates as an independent online learning program and is not affiliated with a school, institution, or other educational platform.
  • Enrollment is voluntary. Students and parents should review the course details, schedule, and fees before applying.
  • Students and parents should not represent the program as part of another institution.

2. Fees, payment & refunds

Payment options

  • Students may select an available monthly or multi-month payment plan, including a six-month option where offered.
  • Course fees are payable in advance for the selected enrollment period.
  • For ongoing monthly enrollment, payment should be completed by the 10th of each month and before participating in classes for that paid period.
  • If fees remain unpaid, class participation and access to learning resources may be paused until payment is completed.

Refund policy

Please choose your payment plan carefully

As we offer a monthly payment option for families who prefer flexibility, all confirmed course payments are final and non-refundable, including payments made for multiple months in advance. This applies if a student later withdraws, misses classes, or chooses not to continue, subject to any rights required by applicable law.

If you have any doubts about a long-term commitment, we warmly encourage you to pay monthly instead.

  • Paying for several months in advance is optional; please select the period that best suits your plans.
  • No cash refund or unused-month adjustment is offered for voluntary early discontinuation, except where required by applicable law.
  • A student who cannot attend temporarily due to examinations or other valid reasons can continue using available recordings and learning resources during the paid enrollment period.
  • Voluntary cancellation or non-renewal does not qualify for a refund, except where required by applicable law. Students retain access to their dashboard and available class recordings, notes, and learning resources until the end of their paid enrollment period.

3. Classes & attendance

  • Classes are currently conducted online.
  • Regular attendance and active participation are expected.
  • Missed classes are not rescheduled individually unless the instructor makes a special arrangement.
  • Class schedules or arrangements may be adjusted when necessary. Significant changes will be communicated to students and parents.

4. Dashboard, recordings & learning materials

  • Each enrolled student receives access to a personal dashboard for available class recordings, notes, and learning resources.
  • Class recordings, live-class content, notes, and other learning materials are provided for the enrolled student's own learning.
  • Sharing, copying, redistributing, or publicly posting class materials or recordings without permission is prohibited.
  • Access to resources is tied to the student's paid enrollment period.

5. Referral discounts

  • For every three new students successfully referred and enrolled, the referring student qualifies for a 20% discount on the next month's fee.
  • For six successful referrals, another 20% discount is applied to the remaining fee, rather than adding the percentages together.
  • For nine successful referrals, a further 20% discount is applied to the new remaining amount.
  • Discounts apply only after the required referrals are successfully enrolled.

6. Quarterly student recognition

  • Learn with Kayes may organize student recognition or gift activities on a quarterly basis.
  • Students may be considered based on performance, discipline, consistency, and overall improvement.
  • Selection and recognition are at the instructor's discretion; an award is not guaranteed.

7. Changes to program policies

  • Learn with Kayes may update class schedules, program rules, or referral and discount policies when required.
  • Students and parents will be informed in advance of significant changes.

Before you submit your application

By selecting the agreement checkbox and submitting the admission form, the student and/or parent or guardian acknowledges that they have read and agree to these Terms & Conditions.

Questions before enrolling? Please use the questions or feedback field on the admission form to clarify anything you are unsure about.
